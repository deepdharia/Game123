## Full description
The number puzzle with REAL math.

1 + 2 = 3. Then 3 + 3 = 6, 6 + 6 = 12… keep doubling all the way to 1536. But watch out — 1s and 2s never merge with themselves, so every move is a real decision. Fans of 2048, Threes, and 2248 will feel right at home, with a twist that makes every board fresh.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe to slide all tiles
• A 1 next to a 2 merges into 3
• Matching tiles 3 and up double: 3+3=6, 6+6=12, up to 1536
• Win when you make 1536 — then keep going in endless mode

FEATURES
• Real arithmetic — no fake merges, the math always checks out
• 4 beautiful themes: Glass, Paper, Neon, Classic
• Daily Challenge — same board for everyone, every day
• 5 undos per game, 4×4 / 5×5 / 6×6 boards
• Best score & lifetime stats, win celebration at 1536
• 100% offline, tiny download, no account needed

If you love merge games, math puzzles, and brain-training number games, 123 is your next obsession. How far past 1536 can you go?
