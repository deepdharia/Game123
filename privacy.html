Privacy Policy — 123

Effective: September 30, 2026

123 ("the app") is a number puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles — in short: almost none.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data in the current version.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, best score, settings, and statistics are saved only in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The current version of the app shows no advertisements. If advertising is introduced in a future update, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and knowingly collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address before the change takes effect in the app.
